# Review checklist

## Per scene (stills at 6–10 points)
- Nothing overlaps: headline vs UI, cards vs edges, tooltips vs text.
- No leftovers from exited elements (underline strokes at mask edges, faded cards).
- The UI matches the product's real copy and states; nothing claims a feature that doesn't exist.
- The accent word and the UI moment land when the VO says them.
- Every sub-navigation that moves (tabs, sidebars) actually changes the content it points at.
- Icons and logos are the official marks, not approximations.

## Full cut
- 1 fps contact sheet: rhythm, variety of moves, no dead frames longer than ~0.5s.
- Transcript of the final mix matches the script (the VO is intelligible over music).
- Loudness −14 LUFS ±1; no music dropouts; intro isn't silent.
- Colours stay in the brand palette.
- Transitions: no hard cut where a wipe/rise was intended.

## Before sharing
- Fictional data only; no real names, emails or photos.
- Thumbnail as frame 0 and as cover art; standalone PNG/JPG for platforms with custom thumbnails.
- Say what changed since the previous version, and what you could not verify (real-time viewing).
